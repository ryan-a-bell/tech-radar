Prusa's fully enclosed CoreXY FDM printer (about 250×210×270 mm). Its actively heated chamber (up to 55 °C) handles ASA/ABS and other engineering filaments, and it's roughly 20% faster than the MK4S. Open-source, screwdriver-repairable, about $1,199 assembled or $949 as a kit, and MK4S owners can convert.
