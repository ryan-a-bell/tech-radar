Bambu Lab's enclosed CoreXY FDM printer that replaces the P1S. Same 256 mm cube build volume, but with a servo extruder, 300 °C quick-swap hotend, AI print-failure detection, and up to four AMS 2 Pro units for multicolor. About $549, or $799 with AMS. Tom's Hardware Editor's Choice.
