Formlabs' benchtop SLS printer: a 30 W fiber laser and a 165×165×300 mm build, printing Nylon 12/11, glass- and carbon-filled nylons, and TPU 90A, with open-material mode. Paired with the Fuse Sift powder station, it's about $24.6k for the printer, and powder starts at $99/kg. The de facto default for in-house SLS.
