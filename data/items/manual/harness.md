Google Research framework that recursively improves an AI agent's harness (system prompt, guardrails, memory, tool guidance, subagents, skills, scripts, plugins) with model weights held fixed, via task-level refinement and a persistent improvement harness that carries lessons across tasks. Runs on OpenCode or Codex executors with JobBench, LAB and WorkBuddy-Bench benchmarks; relevant to agent/skill self-improvement.
