V1 Engineering's open (CC BY-NC-SA) DIY CNC router, now LR4, built from 3D-printed parts, EMT conduit rails, and MGN12 linear rails. It scales from bench-top to a full 4×8 ft sheet for about $400–650 plus a router, and runs Marlin, Grbl, or FluidNC on their Jackpot board.
