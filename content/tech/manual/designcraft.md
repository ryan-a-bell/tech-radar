Clean-room, pure-Rust page-layout app modeled on InDesign: spreads, parent pages, threaded stories, styles, and a Knuth–Plass paragraph composer with hyphenation and optical margin alignment, native and in the browser via WASM, scriptable through the shared Crafting Apps command API.
