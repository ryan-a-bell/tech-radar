Extensible open-source Kubernetes web UI from kubernetes-sigs that runs either in-cluster or as a desktop app against your kubeconfig. It offers multi-cluster views, RBAC-aware actions, logs, an exec terminal, and a resource editor, and you can extend it with plugins.
