Open-source technical data package library from American Intelligence Corporation: 18,685 STEP AP242 solid models, searchable clean copies, and validation records for every public (Distribution A) military-spec part used on U.S. nuclear submarine classes. Generated by agents on the AIC Platform from decades-old scanned spec sheets, it's a large-scale example of agentic document-to-CAD modernization.
