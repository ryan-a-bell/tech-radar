Commercial stock-and-flow system dynamics modeler that also mixes in discrete-event and some agent-based structure, with loop-dominance analysis (Loops That Matter), sensitivity runs, and a Vensim translator. Models can be published as interactive web/desktop 'flight simulator' interfaces or run from Excel.
