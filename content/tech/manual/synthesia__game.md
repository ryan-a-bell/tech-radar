Piano-learning game that teaches with Guitar-Hero-style falling notes: it waits for the right notes, lets you practice each hand separately, and tracks progress over time. Works with MIDI/MusicXML files, optional sheet music, and lighted keyboards; free core version with a $39 unlock.
