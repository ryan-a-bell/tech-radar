Simplified Technical English: a controlled-language standard (53 writing rules + ~900-word approved dictionary) originally built by the aerospace industry for maintenance documentation. Its unambiguous, one-word-one-meaning rules are a promising discipline for writing clear, deterministic LLM prompts and agent instructions.
