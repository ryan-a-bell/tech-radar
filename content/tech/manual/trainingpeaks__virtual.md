Indoor virtual cycling app (formerly indieVelo) for races, group rides, structured workouts and free-roam/GPX routes, with realistic wind and drafting physics; bundled with TrainingPeaks Premium, syncs to Garmin Connect, runs on desktop, mobile and Apple TV, and supports the OpenBikeControl protocol.
