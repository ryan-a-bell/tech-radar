Lean, modern VPN that tunnels IP over UDP using the Noise protocol framework (Curve25519, ChaCha20-Poly1305, BLAKE2), with SSH-style public-key peer config. Born in the Linux kernel and now cross-platform; the protocol underneath Tailscale and most newer mesh VPNs.
