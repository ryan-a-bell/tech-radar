EOS's compact industrial SLS system: a 30 W CO2 laser at up to 5 m/s, a 200×250×330 mm build, and production-grade PA12, PA11, glass-filled, Alumide, and TPU powders. A 600 kg machine aimed at serial production of end-use polymer parts, not a desktop printer.
