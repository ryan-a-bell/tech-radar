Thoughtworks' open-source (AGPL-3.0) generator for interactive technology radars, which reads blips (name, ring, quadrant, isNew, description) from a Google Sheet, CSV, or JSON URL. It's available as a hosted tool or self-hosted via Docker/npm, and the self-hosted version supports custom ring and quadrant names. It's a ready-made alternative or companion renderer for this radar's own web view.
