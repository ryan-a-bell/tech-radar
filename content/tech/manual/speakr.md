Self-hosted web app that turns audio recordings into searchable, organized notes, with pluggable transcription engines (WhisperX, OpenAI, AssemblyAI), speaker diarization, auto-summaries, and chat across a recording or the whole library. Flask/Vue stack deployed via Docker; AGPLv3 with a commercial option.
