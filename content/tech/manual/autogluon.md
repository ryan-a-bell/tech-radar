Open-source (Apache-2.0) AutoML library that trains and stacks/ensembles many models in a few lines of code, with predictors for tabular data, time-series forecasting, and multimodal inputs. A strong baseline-in-minutes for tabular and forecasting problems (e.g. trading/quant feature sets) before hand-tuning.
